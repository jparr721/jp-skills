Outcome honesty

DONE, BLOCKED, and pending stay distinct

DONE

Ready PR, final APPROVE, QA PASS, delivered deck, and required checks completed on the reported head.

BLOCKED

State the blocker and preserve missing or failed evidence. An absent PR, QA pass, or deck can be honest when the task is blocked.

PENDING

Queued CI, unfinished review, unrun QA, or an unresolved run remains pending. Do not convert it into a success claim.

At the review cap, open must-fix findings remain a stop-and-report outcome. They do not become DONE or merge authorization.
